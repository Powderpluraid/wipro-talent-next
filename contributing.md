**🎓 Wipro Talent Next** is the complete English-language guide to the Wipro Talent Next program — a free, industry-aligned learning and hiring initiative for engineering students in India. Learn how to enroll, choose a learning track, prepare for the assessment, build the required capstone project, and convert the program into a job offer. Built for final-year students, college placement officers, and trainers. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.
